# Better Player Enterprise

Better Player Enterprise adds commercial streaming capabilities on top of Better Player: client-side VAST/VMAP ad break scheduling, tracking beacon dispatchers, DRM license automation, and premium UI overlays.

---

## Early Pilot Program

We are looking for companies and production apps to trial enterprise features.

If your team runs video in production and wants features like client-side ad insertion or DRM orchestration, you can join our pilot program:

* **Free for early birds**: During active development of the major feature set, the enterprise license is completely free for pilot partners.
* **Fair expectations**: It will not stay free forever, but early partners get full access at zero cost while we build and refine the engine.
* **Direct feedback loop**: Shape the roadmap, request cue point behaviors, or report edge cases directly.

---

## Package Distribution

The enterprise package is distributed privately outside `pub.dev`. It is not public.

When accepted into the pilot program, your team receives:
* Access to the private enterprise repository and releases
* A signed cryptographic trial license key
* Integration support

---

## How to Get Access

Send an email to **[betterplayer@hasoft.pl](mailto:betterplayer@hasoft.pl)** with:
* Your company or project name
* Platforms you target (Android, iOS, Web, Desktop)
* Features you want to trial (for example: VAST ads, VMAP schedules, DRM)

We will set you up with private access and an early bird trial license.
